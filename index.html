How It Works
📋Draft: Snake draft of 11 players per team. Pool = top players per position per PL club (~300 total).
🗓️Each GW: Everyone simultaneously picks 3 steals from their target + 3 saboteur replacements to plant in that same team. Revealed once all submitted.
🔄Rotation: Round-robin — you face each of the other 5 managers before the cycle repeats.
💸Transfers: One free transfer per GW from undrafted pool. Lowest scorer picks first.
🏆Scoring: Highest FPL points that GW wins. Season total tracked. Base team resets each week.
